BREAKING EXCLUSIVE

VIT Achieves Historic Top Tier in National University Rankings: Research Output Surges by 48%

Vellore Institute of Technology has attained unprecedented global acclaim following the latest institutional audit, securing premier standing among higher technical education universities. The milestone crowns four decades of relentless academic innovation, pioneering interdisciplinary research, and high-impact international consortiums. Across sixteen academic schools, over two thousand research grants were commercialized, yielding patent filings in quantum cryptographic hardware, renewable solar microgrids, and autonomous neural vehicle perception.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Driverless Campus Shuttles Begin Full Autonomous Pilot

The School of Information Technology and Engineering unveiled five all-electric driverless shuttles engineered entirely by undergraduate researchers. Navigating Katpadi Road corridors using solid-state LiDAR and computer vision, the fleet operates 24-hour routes connecting Mens Hostels to the Technology Tower.

CAMPUS SUSTAINABILITY

372-Acre Campus Reaches Net-Zero Carbon Benchmark

A monumental milestone in university green engineering was celebrated yesterday as the Department of Facilities transitioned the final academic quad to 100% rooftop solar energy. In tandem with an automated zero-discharge water recycling plant, campus emissions fell by 82% over the previous fiscal cycle.

STUDENT AFFAIRS

Delegates From 140 Colleges Arrive for Technical Symposium

The registration desk at Anna Convocation Auditorium saw a record influx of three thousand student innovators competing in algorithm marathons, embedded drone sprints, and cybersecurity war-games. Silicon Valley sponsors pledged twenty-five research fellowships for top podium teams.

ATHLETICS & RECREATION

Basketball Squad Captures South Zone Collegiate Championship

Overcoming a ten-point fourth-quarter deficit, the VIT varsity basketball squad defeated Chennai rivals 84-78 in front of an ecstatic four-thousand-strong crowd at the indoor sports complex, qualifying for the upcoming All-India University Finals in New Delhi.

ACADEMIC RESOURCES

Periyar Central Library Unveils 24/7 Digital Commons

A state-of-the-art computational reading commons equipped with gigabit fiber terminals and high-performance GPU nodes opened on the third tier of the library, granting researchers continuous access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and computational clusters.

HEALTH & WELLNESS

Campus Hospital Launches Digital Triage & Telemedicine Desk

The university healthcare division announced full migration to encrypted electronic health records, permitting students to book clinical slots, consult on-call duty officers, and access pharmacy orders instantaneously via the unified Smart Campus web portal.
